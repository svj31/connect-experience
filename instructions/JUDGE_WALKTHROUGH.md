# Judge walkthrough (10 minutes)

Start the app (`npm run setup` once, then `npm run demo`) and open http://localhost:3000.

## 1. Landing page (2 min)
- Hover the logo in the header: the worlds orbit, the heart beats, the spark twinkles.
- Move the mouse over the 3D hero: it tilts. Hover it and the worlds race.
- The cursor is a small world with an orbit ring; over buttons it turns rose with a heart; clicking throws sparks.
- Scroll: the problem, the idea (try the planet builder and the Vibe preview), activities (play a round of
  Would You Rather against a sample partner), bond levels (click through Stardust to Galaxy), and privacy
  (drag the reveal slider).
- Toggle dark mode with the moon icon.

## 2. Member side (5 min)
Click **Try the live demo** (you become Ava).
- **Home**: Question of the day, stats, *Your universe* in Orbit, Cards and Timeline views, picks for you, charts.
- **Demo personas**: on the sign in page, pick Ava, Rohan, Mira, Kabir, Zoya or Arjun to see the app from different sides.
- **Discover**: a swipe deck. Drag or use the buttons (or arrow keys): pass, like, super like, like with a note on a prompt, undo a pass. Kabir and Arjun already liked Ava, so liking them back is an instant match. Browse (with filters) and Galaxy views are one click away; every card says *why* the Vibe score.
- **Connections**: the Likes you tab shows Kabir and Arjun. Like back for the "It's a Saath!" celebration.
  Try the Bond ladder view.
- **Profile of Rohan** (from Connections): Bond orbit, Vibe breakdown, sections that unlock by bond level.
- **Chats**: open Rohan; note the activity chips suggested for the pair.
- **Together**: Talk / Play / Create / Watch, then who with, then locked or free table.
- **Room**: open a private window, sign in as `rohan@saathverse.test` / `saathverse123`, open the same
  room from Connections. Play Deep Talk; chat lives behind the floating button with an unread badge.
- **Moments**, **My profile** (planet builder, preview as a stranger), **Settings** (privacy per section,
  theme, cursor, data export), **Safety centre** (date check-in planner, quick exit).

## 3. Admin side (2 min)
Sign out, then **Sign in > Admin** and **Use the demo admin account**.
- Overview analytics, the report queue (two reports about Dev), Members with masked emails,
  pause and restore, the audit log, and the admin profile.
