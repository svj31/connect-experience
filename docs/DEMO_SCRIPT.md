# Demo video script (about 3 minutes)

**0:00 Problem.** "Dating apps ask you to judge a photo and then type 'hey'. Problem Statement 06 asks for
connection through doing things together. Overlap starts every connection with an activity."

**0:20 Landing.** Show the 3D hero, try the Would You Rather demo, slide the reveal levels.

**0:40 Sign up.** No photo. Build an avatar from a shape and color, pick interests.

**1:00 Lobby.** Open Tables show shared interests. Mention Quick Match and private codes.

**1:15 Two windows, one table.** Would You Rather: picks stay hidden, then flip together. Chat and a reaction
floating over the 3D table. Draw Together live. Connect Four turns.

**1:55 Constellation.** Place stars on the 3D globe from both windows, name it. A bead appears on the thread
between the two seats.

**2:15 Reveal and Common Ground.** Level 1 unlocks after one moment; the partner now sees the bio. Common Ground
shows real counts and sparks; press Use to send one.

**2:35 Safety and quality.** Report and block, privacy controls, account deletion. 98 automated tests including a
two browser end to end date.

**2:50 Close.** "Overlap: meet over something you both enjoy."
